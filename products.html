How to enjoy

Best served with intention

Neat

Sipped slowly at room temperature. Let the agave speak for itself. The truest way to experience Hermanos AVE.

On the rocks

A single large ice cube opens up the Silver's brightness, letting the citrus notes bloom freely in the glass.

In a cocktail

The Silver shines in a Paloma or Margarita — its clean brightness elevating every sip into a celebration.
